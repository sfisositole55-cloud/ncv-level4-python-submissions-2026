Part 2 of 4 Boiler

🔥 Boiler performance

How much of the coal’s energy ends up in the steam? Equivalent evaporation, factor of evaporation and boiler efficiency.

Three measures you must know

Boiler efficiency = ṁs(h − hfw) ÷ (ṁf × CV)
Factor of evaporation = (h − hfw) ÷ 2257
Equivalent evaporation = ṁs × factor of evaporation

Why 2257?It is the latent heat of steam at 100 °C. Equivalent evaporation compares any boiler with a standard one: “from and at 100 °C”, turning water at 100 °C into dry steam at 100 °C.

The Willans line

Steam used by a turbine rises in a straight line with load: ṁ = a + b·P, where a is the no-load steam (kg/h) and b is the extra steam per kW.
